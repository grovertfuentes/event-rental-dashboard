# Email lead counts

Only aggregate counts belong in `public/data/email-counts.json`. Never commit email bodies, addresses, customer names, message IDs, browser sessions, or authentication data.

Version 1 uses `America/New_York` calendar dates. Each unique date has nonnegative integer `wishlists` and `forms`, `status` (`partial`, `current`, or `complete`), and an ISO timestamp with offset in `asOf`. A fully enumerated ongoing-day snapshot is `current` and gives exact counts through its scan time; its date must match the New York scan day. A partial scan establishes a minimum and displays a plus sign. Missing dates or unavailable feeds display an em dash. Weekly comparisons are allowed once every elapsed day has complete coverage; this week so far is compared with the full previous week. Future days remain unknown.
